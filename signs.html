60-second leak test

  1. Turn off every faucet, the dishwasher, the washer and the sprinklers. Make sure no toilet is running.
  2. Open the meter box. It's usually near the street at the front of the yard.
  3. Watch the small triangle or star-shaped dial for 60 seconds. That's the leak indicator. The big sweep hand only shows how much water you've used.

If it turns while everything is off, water is going somewhere. Even a slow turn counts.

Why it happens here

Fort Worth is hard on pipes.

  • The ground moves. North Texas clay shrinks in drought and swells after rain. Slabs shift, and the pipes under them bend and break.
  • A lot of the pipe is old. Many older neighborhoods still run on original galvanized water pipe and cast iron drains. Both wear out from the inside.
