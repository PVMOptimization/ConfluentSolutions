The 60-second leak test

Your water meter can tell you if you have a leak.

  1. Turn off every faucet, the dishwasher, the washer and the sprinklers. Make sure no toilet is running.
  2. Open the meter box, usually near the street at the front of the yard.
  3. Watch the small triangle or star-shaped dial for 60 seconds. That's the leak indicator. The big sweep hand only tells you how much water you've used.

If it turns while everything is off, water is going somewhere. Even a slow turn counts.

What did your leak dial do?

You likely have a leak. If the yard by the meter is dry, it's probably inside the walls or under the slab. If there's a soggy patch outside, check the yard line signs. We've marked it below. A free estimate finds exactly where it is.
Good news: no active leak on the water side right now. Rusty water, slow drains, gas or water heater trouble are separate problems. Check those signs below.
